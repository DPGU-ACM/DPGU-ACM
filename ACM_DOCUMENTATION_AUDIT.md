## F. Conflicts and Uncertainties Register

To preserve rigorous historical accuracy, the following document ambiguities, drafting inconsistencies, and discrepancies are recorded:

1. **Institutional Nomenclature (DPGU vs. DPU vs. DYP DPGU vs. DPG University Gurugram)**:
   - *Conflict*: The primary activity reports use variations such as "Dnyaan Prasad Global University (DPGU)", "Dr. D. Y. Patil Unitech Society - School of Technology and Research", and "Dr. D. Y. Patil Dnyan Prasad University, Pimpri, Pune". Meanwhile, some template files in `DPGU_ACM_WEBSITE` (e.g. `README.md`) refer to "DPG University (Gurugram, India)".
   - *Resolution*: The authoritative official signed activity reports and geocoded photographic evidence (`Lat 18.6228780 Long 73.816370`, Pimpri, Pune 411018) definitively prove that the chapter belongs to **Dnyaan Prasad Global University (DPGU), School of Technology and Research, Pimpri, Pune, Maharashtra**, under the Dr. D. Y. Patil Unitech Society. The Gurugram reference in the web template is a legacy placeholder drafted due to abbreviation overlap.

2. **Inauguration Date vs. Event Schedule**:
   - *Evidence*: `Activity Report on Innovative Idea Exhibition .pdf` states that the event took place in "September 2025 (Monday)" on the occasion of Engineer's Day, and explicitly highlights: *"The inauguration of the ACM Club on the occasion of Engineer's Day added special significance, highlighting the spirit of innovation and technical excellence."*
   - *Analysis*: In India, Engineer's Day is celebrated annually on September 15th. In 2025, September 15th fell precisely on a Monday. This confirms September 15, 2025 as the formal inauguration date of the club.

3. **Section Omission in Women in Leadership Report (`06_Activity Report...`)**:
   - *Observation*: In `06_Activity Report on Panel Discussion on Women in Leadership.pdf`, Page 1 ends at Section 4 (*Objective of the Activity*), and Page 2 opens directly with Section 6 (*Outcomes*), omitting Section 5 (*Activity Details / Panelist Profiles*).
   - *Resolution*: Outcomes and objectives are verified from the document. The specific individual panelist roster for this session is not recorded in the PDF body, unlike the October 2026 AI & Cybersecurity panel which lists all 4 panelists in full. This omission is faithfully maintained without inventing panelist names.

4. **Editorial Artifact in Industry Impact Dialogues (`04_Activity Report...`)**:
   - *Observation*: Section 5 of `04_Activity Report on Industry Impact Dialogues.pdf` starts with the sentence: *"The Innovative Idea Exhibition witnessed participation from multiple colleges including:"* before immediately continuing with the bullet point: *"> The session was conducted as an expert guest lecture under the Industry Impact Dialogues initiative. > The keynote speaker for the session was Mr. Sujit Patange..."*
   - *Resolution*: This is an unedited copy-paste sentence fragment from the earlier exhibition template. The actual event details (Sujit Patange, Vice President Northern Trust, LLMs/ChatGPT in Academics) are completely consistent.

5. **Prize Pool Variations for HELIX 2026**:
   - *Evidence*: The poster content doc (`HELIX HACKATHON POSTER CONTENT.docx`) and sponsor brochure (`HELIX HACKATHON SPONS BROCHURE CONTENT.docx`) mention a prize pool of "Up to ₹40,000", whereas the rulebook (`HELIX HACKATHON RULE BOOK.docx`) cites "Total prize pool: Up to ₹25,000+".
   - *Resolution*: Both are recorded in the audit as target ranges. The actual festival activity report (`09_Helix.pdf`) lists the winning teams across the 4 tracks without specifying the final cash disbursement per team.

6. **External Document: Ignition Hackverse**:
   - *Observation*: Files `Ignition_Hackverse_Brochure.pdf` and `Ignition_Hackverse_Rule_Book.pdf` describe a 24-hour national hackathon on 3–4 April 2026 organized by Pune Vidyarthi Griha's College of Engineering, Technology and Management (PVG's COET&M, Pune).
   - *Resolution*: This is an external event organized by PVGCOET. It is not an event organized by DPGU ACM Student Chapter and is therefore excluded from the chapter's internal event archive.

7. **Tech Treasure Attendance and Results**:
   - *Observation*: `Activity Report on tech treasure.pdf` includes the event poster, dates (15th September 2026), format (TechQuiz + HackerRank Arena), registration cap (first 50), and event photos, but includes a note stating: *"The event poster and supplied draft do not provide confirmed attendance figures, round-wise results, winners, prize distribution, or participant feedback."*
